# Brag Plan: Duxio — The Client Acquisition OS for Coaches

## What is this app?

Duxio is the automated client acquisition operating system for high-ticket coaches ($1,000 to $10,000+ programs) that turns existing audience attention and DMs into a predictable flow of pre-sold, qualified sales calls.

## The angle

The brutal reality of high-ticket coaching: coaches are stuck in "DM Prison" managing spreadsheets, sending manual links, and watching 40%+ of warm leads vanish because prospects buy in 30–90 days, not on Day 1. Duxio replaces fragmented tools with one unified acquisition backend that captures, nurtures, qualifies, and books calls automatically.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

"Likes don't pay bills. Stop losing 40% of your coaching leads to manual DMs."
High-contrast obsidian card with glowing orange accent and the broken "Old Way" stack collapsing.

## Key moments (the middle)

- **Moment 1: The 5-Stage Leak Diagnosis:** Visualizing the 5 points of failure (Traffic, Capture, Nurture, Booking, Follow-up).
- **Moment 2: The $108k/Year Revenue Math:** The interactive calculator calculating 50 inquiries × $3,000 offer = +$9,000/mo ($108,000/year).
- **Moment 3: The Automated Journey:** Attract → Capture → Nurture → Qualify → Book → Convert.

## Outro / punchline

"A fragmented process leaks money. A connected system prints it."
Duxio logo reveal with live badge: "Built for $1k–$10k+ Coaches".

## User flow worth showing

Entry on calculator (50 inquiries × $3k) → Leak identification across 5 stages → Live booking calendar auto-populating with qualified pre-sold buyers.
